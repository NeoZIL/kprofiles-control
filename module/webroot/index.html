How to read this

Frequency values show the current clock converted to MHz; MIN/MAX FREQUENCY are the current allowed policy limits, not the chip’s theoretical maximum. The percentage in brackets is utilization, not frequency as a percentage of maximum. CPU utilization is calculated from successive /proc/stat samples: (change in total CPU counters − change in idle and I/O-wait counters) ÷ change in total CPU counters × 100. For each CPU cluster, the counter changes are combined across the CPUs in that policy. The first sample establishes a baseline, so utilization may show “—%” until a second sample arrives. GPU utilization uses the driver’s busy-percentage value when available; otherwise it is calculated from successive busy/total counter or busy-time/total-time readings. Battery drain in %/h is estimated from the drop in charge-counter relative to full-charge capacity over elapsed time, falling back to battery-percentage change when charge counters are unavailable. Only discharging samples are used; charging periods are excluded. Last valid estimates survive WebUI reopen/reload, while stale active sample windows are discarded and never joined across a screen-off gap. The display may say “Collecting data” until the first reliable estimate exists. Sensor names and availability vary by kernel, so unsupported readings are shown as Unavailable.
